# IDD Demo Review Checklist

Review criteria only — not factual evidence.

## Source Boundary

- Fact claims must be supported by source-role input materials (T0/T1).
- Template, checklist, sample, and reference materials must not be used as factual evidence.
- Sample IDD must not supply F-xx, boundaries, interfaces, or misuse facts for this project.

## ISO 26262-3 Clause 5 Completeness

- Item identification (§5.4.1) covered or explicitly open.
- Functions (§5.4.2) covered or explicitly open.
- System boundary In/Out (§5.4.3) covered or explicitly open.
- External interfaces with direction and counterpart (§5.4.3) covered or explicitly open.
- Operating environment and constraints (§5.4.4) covered or explicitly open.
- Operational situations and modes (factual, no hazard ratings) covered or explicitly open.
- Assumptions and dependencies (§5.4.4) explicit; not mixed with misuse.
- Reasonably foreseeable misuse (§5.4.4 b) in dedicated section or explicitly open.

## IDD Purity

- No hazard, hazardous event, S/E/C, ASIL, safety goal, or risk-acceptability content in IDD.
- No「定义已批准」「已满足 ISO 26262 合规」「可量产」等 forbidden final claims.

## Delivery Boundary

- Unsupported claims remain `NEEDS_USER_CONFIRMATION` or open.
- Final report is review-ready, not professional sign-off.
- Candidate learning artifacts remain proposed and inactive.
