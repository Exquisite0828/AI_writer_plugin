# Technical Solution Checklist

- The document follows the required template sections.
- Architecture decisions are supported by source material or marked NEEDS_USER_CONFIRMATION.
- Performance targets are supported by source material or marked NEEDS_USER_CONFIRMATION.
- Security boundaries are supported by source material or marked NEEDS_USER_CONFIRMATION.

- Rollout risks and rollback plan are addressed.
