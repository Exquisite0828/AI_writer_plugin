# HARA Demo Review Checklist

## Source Boundary

- Fact claims must be supported by source-role input materials.
- Template, checklist, sample, and expected output shape materials must not be used as factual evidence.
- Method references may support methodology context but must not be treated as project-specific EPS facts.

## HARA Judgment Boundary

- Hazard identification requires qualified human confirmation.
- Hazardous event definition requires qualified human confirmation.
- Severity, exposure, and controllability ratings require qualified human confirmation.
- ASIL or risk classification requires qualified human confirmation.
- Safety goal wording requires qualified human confirmation.

## Delivery Boundary

- Weak evidence must be carried forward instead of silently resolved.
- Unsupported claims must be marked or omitted.
- Missing or unsupported materials must remain visible in final delivery if present.
- Candidate learning artifacts must remain proposed and inactive.
