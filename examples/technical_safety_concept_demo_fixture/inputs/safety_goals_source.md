# Confirmed Safety Goals

Document status: project source for this demo fixture.

## Safety Goal SG-01

Safety goal identifier: SG-01.
Safety goal statement: Prevent unintended steering assist torque that could cause loss of intended steering path during low-speed parking assist.
ASIL: ASIL B.
Safe state: Remove automated parking assist torque request and keep manual steering assist available.

## Safety Goal SG-02

Safety goal identifier: SG-02.
Safety goal statement: Prevent continued automated parking assist operation when required steering sensor inputs are unavailable or implausible.
ASIL: ASIL B.
Safe state: Degrade parking assist and notify the driver.
